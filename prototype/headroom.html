Headroom — screen prototype

Three destinations: Brief, Commitments, Ledger. Voice is an overlay, not a tab. Commitment detail is a push. Light mode, white / near-black, single indigo accent.

The assistant is Otto. Onboarding (8–10) is a three-card conversation, not a form: Otto introduces itself, asks your name and one line about what you do, then hands off to setup. Anything the device already knows — your name from Google, your timezone — is shown for correction rather than asked. Screen 15 is why it matters: an empty graph reading as someone waiting instead of a blank page.
